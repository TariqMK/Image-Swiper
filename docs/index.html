Nothing changes until you press Delete

Keeping, browsing, changing the order and On This Day never touch your files. Deleted files go to the Recycle Bin or Trash, and on Windows Undo brings the last one back.

Your photos stay on your computer

Image Swiper doesn't connect to the internet and never writes into your photo folders. It keeps file dates and video previews in its own cache.

Opens what your camera makes

JPEG, HEIC and camera RAW files such as CR3, NEF and ARW, plus MP4, MOV and other videos. Dates come from each file's own capture information.

A whole folder, one decision at a time

Press W to keep and Q to delete. The strip under each photo shows where you are in the folder and what you've removed so far.

The contact strip

One block per file. Grey for files you've passed, red for files you deleted, teal for where you are now. Click anywhere on the strip to jump there.

On This Day

Shows only the photos and videos taken on today's date, in any year. Use the arrows to go back or forward a day and catch up on days you missed.

On This Day

  • Keep or delete

    Keep with W, delete with Q, or click the buttons.

  • Undo

    Ctrl Z or Backspace restores the last deleted file on Windows.

  • Your order

    Name, Date or Shuffle. Names sort naturally, so IMG_9 comes before IMG_10.

  • Subfolders

    Leave it on to include every folder inside the one you open.

  • Recent folders

    The arrow beside Open Folder… reopens one of your last three folders.

  • Years ago

    Each photo shows the date it was taken and how many years ago that was.

  • Videos

    Click a video to play it in your default player. FFmpeg adds previews.

  • Camera RAW

    CR2, CR3, NEF, ARW, DNG and ORF open alongside JPEG and HEIC.

  • Open the folder

    Click a photo's folder to open it in Explorer or Finder.

  • Edge clicks

    Click the left or right edge of a photo to go back or forward.

  • Space freed

    A running count of files deleted and space sent to the bin.

  • Fast return visits

    Dates are read once and cached, so a folder opens faster next time.

Download

Free. Extract the download to a folder you can write to, such as Documents, and keep everything in it together. Nothing else to install.

Windows

64-bit · ZIP

Open ImageSwiper.exe. If SmartScreen says it protected your PC, click More info, then Run anyway.

macOS

Apple silicon · ZIP

Open ImageSwiper.app. If it won't open, go to System Settings › Privacy & Security and click Open Anyway. Intel Macs aren't supported.

Linux

x64 · TAR.GZ

Run ImageSwiper/ImageSwiper from a desktop session. Ubuntu 22.04 or newer is supported.

Image Swiper isn't signed, so your computer may warn you the first time. For video previews, install FFmpeg. To run from source, see the development notes.
